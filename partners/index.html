Partner categories

The four partner categories.

01
FxPro Associate

Independent professionals and business contacts

Refer companies that need FX optimisation, export-import support, trade finance or treasury services. Earn approved referral rewards for qualifying business.

02
FxPro Professional Partner

Eligible CAs, consultants and trade professionals

Develop a structured referral relationship with lead registration, defined commercial terms and periodic performance reviews.

03
FxPro Strategic Partner

Eligible agencies, business networks and advisory firms

Build a continuing pipeline through formal partnerships, industry associations, business communities and approved co-marketing.

04
FxPro Confidential Lead Network

People who prefer not to be publicly associated with a referral

Submit a business lead discreetly. Initial lead intelligence can be accepted without an identity, but it is unpaid until a lawful, eligible and verifiable referral arrangement is established. This is not a channel for concealed commissions to bank employees.

How it works

Referring takes less than a minute.

1. Spot an opportunity

An eligible partner identifies a business that may benefit from FxPro.

2. Register the lead

Submit the company name, public website and broad requirement using a secure form or unique referral link.

3. FxPro takes ownership

The sales team validates the lead, checks existing relationships and manages all prospect communication.

4. Track the outcome

The partner sees progress through a private reference number without accessing confidential client details.

5. Receive the reward

Once the client generates eligible revenue and all verification, documentation and payment conditions are met, the reward is calculated and paid on the agreed cycle.

All rewards are subject to eligibility, written terms, applicable law, professional obligations and compliance approval. Confidentiality does not override employer policies or legal disclosure requirements.
